# User Manual

1. **Account Creation:** Launch the app and register a new account using your email address. Log in to access the main dashboard.
2. **Starting a Session:** Select your desired focus duration (15, 25, or 60 minutes) from the main menu.
3. **The Physical Trigger:** Place your phone face down on a desk. The app will detect the movement and begin the countdown automatically.
4. **Staying Focused:** Do not pick up the device. If the phone is lifted beyond a safe threshold, the session will cancel.
5. **Progress:** Upon hearing the completion vibration, pick up your phone. Your earned points will be synced to your profile.
